# Advanced Topics

## Character and Creature Design

| Subject | Why study? | Book | Online Course |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Character Design | Develop expressive, varied characters with personality, silhouette, and narrative appeal. | [*How to Draw Comics the Marvel Way*](https://archive.org/details/HowToDrawComicsTheMarvelWay) (focus on dynamic figures and design principles; adaptable for characters). | [character design course - Kavya Rao](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL7PRaGopCiYLpDkt6fqe2N6KnLZlIvMrD) OR [Character Design - Christophe Young](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLvRD_ud2ieXdSc_6vj-Fa-qE8Wk05dTvj) OR [KNKL Character Design BootCamp (Full)](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLnF2KxzinVX1FHWsWavMXDU_jqxdeMrtK) |
| Animal Drawing | Learn gesture, anatomy variations, and locomotion for realistic or fantastical creatures as a foundation for design. | [*The Weatherly Guide to Drawing Animals*](https://www.amazon.com/Weatherly-Guide-Drawing-Animals/dp/097103141X) | [Drawing Animals for Beginners - Part 1 - Structure & Form](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mA77noU56_8) + [Drawing Animals for Beginners - Part 2 - Action, Foreshortening & Details](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bPWLFp-XpmU) |
| Creature Design | Combine anatomy, imagination, and variation for original designs, blending animal and character elements. | *Creature Design with Aaron Blaise* | [Creature Design Lessons - Draw Sessions](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLgRfjS8fHJA8nAeP4ZyEXXUw0jzZUij_P) AND/OR [Concept Art Creature Painting](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLnqqJ8abE7KGQHpt8gTzcg-qwJtYluQKa) |
