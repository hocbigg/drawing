## Anatomy & Figure Drawing (Free Books & PDFs)

- [Andrew Loomis - Fun With a Pencil](https://archive.org/details/andrew-loomis-fun-with-a-pencil) - Free classic beginner-friendly drawing fundamentals book.
- [Andrew Loomis - Creative Illustration](https://archive.org/details/andrewloomiscreative.illustration/) - Free Loomis illustration and figure drawing techniques.
- [FORCE Drawing Books](https://www.drawingforce.com/books-page-1) - Free Michael Mattesi dynamic figure drawing book series.
- [Bridgman - Complete Guide to Drawing from Life](https://ia601603.us.archive.org/20/items/pdfy-72f-FzW7wYN_r0ny/Bridgman%20-%20Complete%20Guide%20to%20Drawing%20from%20Life_text.pdf) - Free classic anatomy and figure construction reference.
- [Anatomy for Sculptors](https://anatomy4sculptors.com/) - Free anatomy reference site aimed at sculptors and artists.
- [Atlas of Human Anatomy for the Artist (Peck)](https://archive.org/details/purplerosepetals/) - Free detailed anatomical reference atlas.
- [Burne Hogarth - Drawing Dynamic Hands](https://archive.org/details/BurneHogarthDrawingDynamicHands) - Free hand anatomy and construction guide.
- [Burne Hogarth - Dynamic Figure Drawing (PDF)](https://artesplasticasydiseno.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/burne-hogarth-dynamic-figure-drawing.pdf) - Free dynamic approach to figure construction.
- [How to Draw and Paint Anatomy](https://archive.org/details/how-to-draw-and-paint-anatomy_202404) - Free anatomy drawing and painting guide.
- [Sheppard - Anatomy: A Complete Guide for Artists](https://dn790006.ca.archive.org/0/items/Anatomy-A_Complete_Guide_for_Artists_Joseph_Sheppard/Anatomy-A_Complete_Guide_for_Artists_Joseph_Sheppard.pdf) - Free comprehensive anatomy reference for artists.
- [Hampton - Figure Drawing: Design and Invention](https://cursoseorientacoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/michael-hampton-figure-drawing-design-and-invention-1.pdf) - Free popular modern figure construction method.
- [Vilppu Drawing Manual](https://archive.org/details/Vilppu_Drawing_Manual.pdf) - Free Glenn Vilppu classic figure drawing manual.
- [Color and Light (James Gurney)](https://ia902907.us.archive.org/28/items/Color_and_Light_James_Gurney_English/Color_and_Light_James_Gurney_English.pdf) - Free practical guide to color and light theory for representational art.
- [Simply Drawing](https://archive.org/details/simplydrawing0000gran) - Free beginner drawing instruction book.
- [Mastering Manga](https://archive.org/details/isbn_9781440309311) - Free step-by-step manga drawing instruction.
- [The Animator's Survival Kit](https://archive.org/details/TheAnimatorsSurvivalKitRichardWilliams) - Free Richard Williams definitive animation principles guide.
- [Drawing Basics and Video Game Art](https://archive.org/details/drawingbasicsvid0000sola) - Free Chris Solarski book connecting drawing fundamentals to game art.
