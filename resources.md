## Fundamentals & General Courses

- [Ctrl+Paint](https://www.ctrlpaint.com/) - Huge free tutorial library covering the fundamentals of digital art.
- [Drawabox](https://drawabox.com/) - Free structured course of basic drawing exercises, from fundamentals to figures and environments.
- [Proko](https://www.proko.com/library/) - Video lessons focused on figure drawing and human anatomy.
- [Digital Painting Studio](https://digitalpaintingstudio.com/courses/digital-painting-studio-pro) - Concept art courses, with free intro lessons on Photoshop and art fundamentals.
- [Art Fundamentals Playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLVgLT-e3jXPDgeED0pD0BPq8kY1VAZAGa) - YouTube playlist covering the basics of drawing and painting.

- [CGCookie](https://cgcookie.com/) - Courses on Blender, 2D art, concept art, and anatomy.
- [Schoolism](http://www.schoolism.com/) - Online art courses taught by industry professionals.

- [Nathan Aardvark's Daily Tutorials](https://www.patreon.com/nathanaardvark) - Short, focused daily drawing tutorials.

- [Draw Right](https://www.drawright.com/) - Free structured drawing lessons for beginners.
- [Iterative Drawing](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k0ufz75UvHs) - Video on improving through repeated iteration.

## Anatomy & Figure Drawing (Books & PDFs)

- [Andrew Loomis - Fun With a Pencil](https://archive.org/details/andrew-loomis-fun-with-a-pencil) - Classic beginner-friendly drawing fundamentals book.
- [Andrew Loomis - Creative Illustration](https://archive.org/details/andrewloomiscreative.illustration/) - Loomis's illustration and figure drawing techniques.
- [FORCE Drawing Books](https://www.drawingforce.com/books-page-1) - Michael Mattesi's dynamic figure drawing book series.
- [Bridgman - Complete Guide to Drawing from Life](https://ia601603.us.archive.org/20/items/pdfy-72f-FzW7wYN_r0ny/Bridgman%20-%20Complete%20Guide%20to%20Drawing%20from%20Life_text.pdf) - Classic anatomy and figure construction reference.
- [Anatomy for Sculptors](https://anatomy4sculptors.com/) - Anatomy reference site aimed at sculptors and artists.
- [Atlas of Human Anatomy for the Artist (Peck)](https://archive.org/details/purplerosepetals/) - Detailed anatomical reference atlas.
- [Burne Hogarth - Drawing Dynamic Hands](https://archive.org/details/BurneHogarthDrawingDynamicHands) - Hand anatomy and construction guide.
- [Burne Hogarth - Dynamic Figure Drawing (PDF)](https://artesplasticasydiseno.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2012/12/burne-hogarth-dynamic-figure-drawing.pdf) - Dynamic approach to figure construction.
- [How to Draw and Paint Anatomy](https://archive.org/details/how-to-draw-and-paint-anatomy_202404) - Anatomy drawing and painting guide.
- [Sheppard - Anatomy: A Complete Guide for Artists](https://dn790006.ca.archive.org/0/items/Anatomy-A_Complete_Guide_for_Artists_Joseph_Sheppard/Anatomy-A_Complete_Guide_for_Artists_Joseph_Sheppard.pdf) - Comprehensive anatomy reference for artists.
- [Hampton - Figure Drawing: Design and Invention](https://cursoseorientacoes.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/09/michael-hampton-figure-drawing-design-and-invention-1.pdf) - Popular modern figure construction method.
- [Vilppu Drawing Manual](https://archive.org/details/Vilppu_Drawing_Manual.pdf) - Glenn Vilppu's classic figure drawing manual.
- [Color and Light (James Gurney)](https://ia902907.us.archive.org/28/items/Color_and_Light_James_Gurney_English/Color_and_Light_James_Gurney_English.pdf) - Practical guide to color and light theory for representational art.

- [Perspective Without Pain](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0891344462/) - Simplified introduction to perspective drawing.
- [Keys to Drawing](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0891343377/) - Bert Dodson's approach to observational drawing.
- [The Creative License](http://www.amazon.com/dp/1401307922/) - Danny Gregory's book on creative confidence through drawing.
- [Drawing by Seeing](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0810991705/) - Observation-based drawing instruction.
- [Zen of Seeing](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0394719689/) - Drawing and seeing as a meditative practice.
- [The Natural Way to Draw](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0395530075/) - Kimon Nicolaides' classic drawing study plan.
- [Dynamic Figure Drawing](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0823015777/) - Figure drawing techniques focused on dynamic poses.
- [Dynamic Anatomy](http://www.amazon.com/dp/0823015521/) - Burne Hogarth's anatomy reference, revised edition.
- [Simply Drawing](https://archive.org/details/simplydrawing0000gran) - Beginner drawing instruction book.
- [50 Things to Draw](https://www.amazon.com/50-Things-Draw-Creative-Projects/dp/1435146115) - Prompt-based beginner drawing projects.
- [Beginner's Guide to Digital Painting in Photoshop](https://www.amazon.com/gp/product/0955153077) - 3DTotal's intro to digital painting in Photoshop.
- [Sketch with Asia](https://www.amazon.com/Sketch-Asia-Manga-inspired-Tutorials-Illustrator/dp/1909414662) - Manga-inspired character sketching tutorials.
- [Drawing Animals](https://www.amazon.com/Drawing-Animals-Amazingly-Christopher-Paperback/dp/B00ZM2P4TS) - Guide to drawing animals accurately.
- [Mastering Manga](https://archive.org/details/isbn_9781440309311) - Step-by-step manga drawing instruction.
- [Digital Manga: Composition and Perspective](https://www.amazon.com/Digital-Manga-Composition-Perspective-Artists/dp/4805317922) - Manga-focused composition and perspective guide.
- [Paleoart: The Palaeoartist's Handbook](https://www.amazon.com/Palaeoartists-Handbook-Recreating-Prehistoric-Animals/dp/1785004611) - Guide to drawing prehistoric animals accurately.
- [The Animator's Survival Kit](https://archive.org/details/TheAnimatorsSurvivalKitRichardWilliams) - Richard Williams' definitive animation principles guide.
- [Drawing Basics and Video Game Art](https://archive.org/details/drawingbasicsvid0000sola) - Chris Solarski's book connecting drawing fundamentals to game art.
- [Dracopedia](https://www.amazon.com/Dracopedia-Guide-Drawing-Dragons-World/dp/1600613152) - Guide to designing and drawing dragons.
- [Drawing Cutting-Edge Anatomy](https://www.amazon.com/Drawing-Cutting-Edge-Anatomy-Reference/dp/0823023982) - Advanced anatomy reference for artists.
- [Draw Real People](https://www.amazon.com/Draw-Real-People-Discover-Drawing/dp/0891346570) - Guide to drawing realistic portraits and figures.
- [Life Drawing: How to Portray the Figure with Accuracy and Expression](https://www.amazon.com/Life-Drawing-Portray-Accuracy-Expression/dp/1581809794) - Figure drawing instruction book.
