  • Mastering Manga - Free step-by-step manga drawing instruction.
  • The Animator's Survival Kit - Free Richard Williams definitive animation principles guide.
  • Drawing Basics and Video Game Art - Free Chris Solarski book connecting drawing fundamentals to game art.
